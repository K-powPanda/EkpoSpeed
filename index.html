The Program

What You'll Develop

Train Like Elite.

Every session is built around the physical traits that separate good athletes from great ones. This isn't general fitness — it's targeted athletic development.

⚡
Sprint Mechanics
Master proper acceleration technique, arm drive, and stride mechanics. Run faster by running smarter — form correction is built into every session.
🚀
Explosive Power
Develop first-step quickness and reactive power through bounds, plyometrics, and falling starts — the tools that translate directly to your sport.
🔄
Agility & Change of Direction
Sport-specific lateral movement, shuffles, and direction-change drills for football and baseball athletes who need more than straight-line speed.
🏃
Top End Speed
Push past your current ceiling. Structured max-velocity work builds the top speed that wins races, breaks tackles, and turns singles into doubles.
🛡️
Injury Prevention
Dynamic warm-ups, proper mechanics, and progressive loading protect your body so you stay healthy and training consistently all season long.
📈
Performance Feedback
Real coaching, real reps, real feedback. Every session includes coached reps and direct instruction — not just workouts you could find on YouTube.
